The Market

Buy and sell with the whole world without leaving your depot. Post an offer, set your price, and pick up what you bought or earned in your inbox.

  • Trade with everyone

    Sell to and buy from every player, right at the depot. Browse the offers for any item and take the price you like.

  • Your offer, your price

    Post sell or buy offers, anonymously if you prefer. A small fee per listing keeps the books clean.

  • Every item, every rarity

    Dozens of categories from Common to Legendary, with 30 days of price history for each item.

  • Safe from start to finish

    You pay with the gold in your depot and collect in your inbox. A trade goes through whole or not at all.

In client v0.0.20 : stand at any depot and press Market in the top bar.

Quick setup

Enter Opengea in 3 steps

The launcher installs, updates and verifies the game for you, so you are always on the latest client.

  1. 01

    1. Download the launcher

    Get ClientLauncher.exe (66 MB, Windows x64) from the official GitHub releases. No .NET install needed.

  2. 02

    2. Open it and install

    It installs itself for your user (no admin), then downloads client v0.0.20 (662 MB) and checks it against its SHA-256.

  3. 03

    3. Create your account and play

    Create your account right inside the game client, sign in and play. The launcher keeps the client up to date and can verify or repair your files at any time.
