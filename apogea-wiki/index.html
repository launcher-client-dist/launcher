Community wikis and databases

  • apogeawiki.info: items, monsters, NPC shops and quest guides.
  • Apogea Fandom wiki: spells, classes and general pages.
  • apogean.eu: monster stats, spells and an interactive map.

These are made by players, not by Opengea. Some values can differ on our server, where fixes and balance changes are announced on the Discord.

When the wiki is not enough

Open a ticket on the Opengea Discord: the team checks the game itself and answers with pictures of the place, the item or the NPC.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an interactive Apogea map?+

Yes, apogean.eu has one. In tickets on the Discord, the team also sends map pictures and in-game screenshots of the spot you ask about.

Where can I see who sells an item?+

The wikis list NPC shops. On Opengea you can also check the Market at any depot for player offers and 30 days of price history.
