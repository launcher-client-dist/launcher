Apogea: the medieval fantasy MMORPG

Apogea is a medieval fantasy MMORPG with a pixel-art 3D world seen from above: you explore a large world, take on quests, hunt monsters and bosses with friends, and trade with other players. On Opengea you can play it now, free.

What you do in Apogea

  • Quests across towns, caves, swamps, deserts and dungeons, many of them puzzles with levers, keys and hidden passages.
  • Hunts and bosses: group up to take on the hardest monsters for their loot.
  • Classes and traits: start as a Squire, become a Knight, Mage or Rogue, spend stat points and build your trait tree.
  • Crafting and cooking: forge gear, upgrade items and cook food that gives buffs.
  • Market: buy and sell with every player right from your depot.
  • Mounts: earn the beast tamer's trust and ride.
  • Optional PvP for those who want it.

Is Apogea like Tibia?

Players often compare it to classic MMORPGs like Tibia: a top-down world, careful hunts, quests to discover and a strong community. It has its own world, classes, traits and items.
